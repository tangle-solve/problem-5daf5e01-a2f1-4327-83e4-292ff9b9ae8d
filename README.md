### CHEM-000304: Localizing three or more adjacent mucin O-glycans
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Assign individual glycan compositions to three or more nearby serine/threonine residues with calibrated confidence, separating isomers having identical total composition.
- **Status:** The 2026 DeepGPO framework supports singly and doubly glycosylated peptides. Its authors identify three-plus glycans as beyond current capability because of spectral complexity and scarce training data.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Resolves dense site patterns defining mucin surfaces.
- **Key works:** Y. Zong, Y. Wang and L. Qiao (2026), *Deep Learning Prediction of O-Glycopeptide Tandem Mass Spectra Enhances O-Glycoproteomics*, [DOI:10.1002/advs.76760](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.76760).
